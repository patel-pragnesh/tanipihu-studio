Our Story

Welcome to Tani Pihu Treasure — a little world filled with handmade love, creativity, and thoughtful gifting.

What started as a passion for creating cute and meaningful handmade pieces slowly turned into a dream to spread happiness through every order we make. From adorable crochet keychains and phone charms to customized hampers and party gifting ideas, every product is carefully handcrafted with love, patience, and attention to detail.

At Tani Pihu Treasure, we believe gifts should feel personal and memorable. Whether it is a birthday surprise, kitty party return gift, baby shower favor, festive hamper, or a small token for someone special — we create treasures that bring smiles.

Each piece is packed with care and designed to make your moments extra special. Thank you for supporting our small handmade business and being a part of our journey.
